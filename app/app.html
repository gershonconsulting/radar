Install & update

Takes about a minute — no Chrome Web Store needed.
  1. Download the zip above and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top-right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  5. Come back here and hit Test installation.
Already installed? To update: unzip the new download over the old folder, then click Reload on “Radar — Sales Nav Collector” in chrome://extensions.
Keep Chrome running and stay logged into Sales Navigator — the extension collects on its schedule in the background.
