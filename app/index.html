Install in about a minute
  1. Download the zip above and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top-right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  5. Open Radar → Extension and hit Test installation.
Already installed? The Extension page inside Radar checks your version, your Sales Navigator session and your schedule — and lets you launch a collection on the spot.
