Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how Radar (the web app at radar.gershon.ai and radar.gershoncrm.com) and the Radar — Prospect Collector Chrome extension (together, "Radar") handle data. Radar is operated by Gershon Consulting LLC, a Delaware company ("we", "us").

In short: Radar collects business-profile information from LinkedIn Sales Navigator pages that you, the signed-in user, can already see, and stores it in your own Radar account so you can find warm introductions to your ideal customers. We do not sell data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not read your browsing outside the sites listed below.

1. Who Radar is for and what it does

Radar is a business-development tool. You choose organizations to watch ("sources") and people in your network ("bridges"). The extension then reads, inside your own Sales Navigator session, the professional connections of those bridges that match your ideal-customer filters, and adds them to your Radar account as "prospects".

3. How we use data

  • To provide Radar's features to you: collecting, grading and showing prospects, reports and email summaries about your account.
  • To keep the service safe and working (rate limits, error monitoring, support).
  • We do not sell or rent data, use it for advertising, use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending, or transfer it to data brokers.
  • Human access is limited to Gershon Consulting staff who need it to support your account, or where required by law.

Radar's use of data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. What the extension accesses in your browser

  • linkedin.com — reads Sales Navigator search and lead pages in your own session, at a human pace and within daily limits you control.
  • radar.gershon.ai / radar.gershoncrm.com — lets the Radar web app talk to the extension (status, version, "sync now").
  • Radar Supabase project — https://pkzeeqehwmtnqxdpdesl.supabase.co. The extension sends profile records, bridge/source metadata, owner identifiers, settings and diagnostic logs to /functions/v1/hub. It sends action type, units, extension version and account identifier to /functions/v1/radar-gate to enforce collection limits.
  • enricherpro.com/api/enrich (Gershon's service) — receives first name, last name, company and title to look up a missing public LinkedIn profile URL.
  • api.botdog.co/v1/leads/add_to_campaign — configured outreach sends LinkedIn profile URLs, names, titles and campaign identifiers. The extension can call Botdog with a locally stored API key or ask the hub to use server-stored credentials. Some backend push paths do not yet honor the dashboard Botdog switch consistently; do not rely on that switch alone to prevent configured outreach.

Extension permissions are used only for these purposes: scripting and the LinkedIn host permission to read Sales Navigator pages (opening and closing collection tabs does not require the broad tabs permission), alarms to schedule collection runs, storage to keep settings and progress locally, and notifications to tell you when a run needs your attention.

Before collection starts, the extension requires your explicit acceptance of a disclosure naming these data and destinations. Acceptance and its timestamp are stored locally. Without acceptance, background network requests and collection actions stay blocked. This also applies when an older installation updates without an existing acceptance record. You can leave collection off by closing the disclosure, or withdraw acceptance by clearing extension data or uninstalling. Configured collection may save profiles to LinkedIn lists and send bridge connection invitations, including a configured invitation note; requests use your existing signed-in LinkedIn session, and session/CSRF values stay with LinkedIn rather than being sent to the Radar backend.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Radar, each bound to process data only on our instructions: Supabase (database and backend functions), Cloudflare (website hosting and AI features), Resend (transactional email), LinkedIn (sign-in), and, only if you enable it, Botdog. No data is shared with anyone else except where required by law.

6. Storage, security and retention

  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored in access-controlled databases; each account can only see its own data.
  • We keep your data while your account is active. When you close your account, we delete your account and prospect data within 30 days, except where we must keep records by law.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can pause collection, remove sources, bridges or prospects, or uninstall the extension at any time.
  • You can ask for a copy of your data, correction, or deletion by emailing support@gershonconsulting.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • If someone whose business profile appears in Radar wants it removed, they can write to the same address and we will delete it and stop collecting it.
  • Residents of the EU/UK, California and other regions with data-protection laws have the rights those laws provide (access, deletion, correction, objection); contact us to exercise them.

8. Children

Radar is a professional tool and is not directed to anyone under 18.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell signed-in users in the app or by email.
