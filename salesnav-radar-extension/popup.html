Radar Collector

Collects prospects from your radar bridges in Sales Navigator and syncs them to your Radar account. Bridge discovery for new sources happens automatically as part of the sync.
⏰ Runs automatically once a day while Chrome is open. Use Sync Now to run it on demand.

Allow Radar collection?

Radar reads business profiles and connections visible in your signed-in LinkedIn session: names, titles, companies, locations, profile URLs, member IDs and connection degrees.

It sends these records, account identifiers, settings and diagnostic logs to the Radar Supabase project at pkzeeqehwmtnqxdpdesl.supabase.co to build your prospect list and enforce collection limits. Logs can include profile names, IDs and errors.

Missing profile URLs may be resolved by sending name, company and title to enricherpro.com/api/enrich. Configured Botdog outreach can send profile URLs, names, titles and a campaign ID to api.botdog.co/v1/leads/add_to_campaign, directly or through the backend. Settings may also enable LinkedIn list saves and bridge invitations.

Collection stays off until you accept, including after updating an older installation. Acceptance is saved locally with a timestamp. Close this window to leave collection off. To withdraw consent, uninstall Radar or clear its extension data.
